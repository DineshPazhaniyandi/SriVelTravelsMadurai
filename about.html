About us

A Madurai-based taxi hire desk. We send cars with drivers — not sightseeing itineraries.

What we do

SriVel Travels supplies chauffeur-driven cars for daily city duty, railway and airport transfers, and outstation drop or round trips. You tell us headcount, luggage and time; we match a sedan, SUV, Innova Crysta or tempo traveller.

Fares are published on the tariff page so you can compare cars before you call. Tolls, parking and extra hours are billed as listed — we do not bundle them into a vague “package tour” price.

Drivers work on shift for local duty and on kilometer + bata for highway trips. Vehicles are AC unless you ask otherwise.

Service coverage

  • Madurai city and suburbs
  • Madurai airport pickup / drop
  • Railway station duty
  • Outstation: one-way or return
  • Weddings and event point-to-point drops
